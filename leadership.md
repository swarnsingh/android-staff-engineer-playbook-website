# Leadership

## Purpose

Technical leadership and the operational dimensions of seniority — incident response, on-call, cross-team influence. Incident response is the core Staff signal here.

## Why It Matters

Staff is partly a leadership role. How you behave during incidents, how you run on-call, how you write retrospectives — these are the moments where the title is earned.

## Learning Order

Begin with incident response and on-call as the operational baseline. Then study design reviews and influence without authority, followed by mentorship and escalation. That order mirrors how Staff engineers first stabilize systems, then scale judgment across teams.
