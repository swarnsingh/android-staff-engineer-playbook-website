# Platform Engineering

## Purpose

Internal platforms, developer experience, and the systems that make other teams faster.

## Why It Matters

Staff Mobile Platform Engineers spend most of their time building leverage — not features. Platform engineering is the discipline of building leverage explicitly.

## What This Folder Helps You Do

- Build paved roads that let many product teams ship faster without each team reinventing build, release, design-system, SDK, and dependency patterns.
- Reason about developer experience as a production system: build latency, CI reliability, version governance, documentation, ownership, adoption, and support load.
- Design internal platforms that are useful enough to be adopted voluntarily and opinionated enough to prevent architectural drift.
- Evaluate platform work by leverage: fewer repeated decisions, faster feedback loops, safer migrations, and clearer ownership boundaries.
