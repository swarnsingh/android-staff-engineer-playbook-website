# Platform Engineering

## Purpose

Internal platforms, developer experience, and the systems that make other teams faster.

## Why It Matters

Staff Mobile Platform Engineers spend most of their time building leverage — not features. Platform engineering is the discipline of building leverage explicitly.

## Learning Order

Start with internal developer platforms, because paved roads define how other teams experience architecture. Then study design systems, library versioning, and SDK distribution. Finish with build graph scaling, where platform choices become daily developer productivity.
