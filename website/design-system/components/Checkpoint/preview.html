A click handler calls service.isPlaying() on the main thread and the screen freezes. Where is the time going?

Answer

In the other process. The proxy marshals the call and the main thread waits until a Binder thread in the service runs it and replies. A busy or deadlocked service turns a one-line getter into a long main-thread wait.

What does oneway remove from the caller's wait?

Answer

Waiting for the service to finish the work. The transaction still has to be marshalled and delivered.
