Built for reading

Inter carries headings, labels, tables and the interface. Lesson prose uses the reader's chosen font, Modern Sans by default, at 18px with a 1.7 line height, filling the established760px reading column. JetBrains Mono keeps code distinct with ligatures off. Relationship maps and transfer scenes use Inter labels. The reading sheet is880px. Padded, rounded visual frames may extend symmetrically up to1000px, bounded by the viewport and navigation rails; complex diagrams preserve readable labels and relationships through local scrolling on phones.

Problem, relationships and visible consequences

Read the problem first, then follow labelled arrowheads and owner boundaries. Use Next to inspect a consequence; named steps let you compare states. Play and Pause control the sequence. Reduced motion keeps every state and caption available.

Interview walkthroughs

System-design chapters written as timed interviews keep their full text. The InterviewWalkthrough component derives a timeline from the numbered sections and their time hints, restyles trade-off tables as decision cards and labels follow-up questions as pushback checks. These samples come from Offline Sync.

The website standard

Each lesson starts from a problem, builds a mental model, reveals the mechanism and its failure modes, then asks the reader to reason. Shared phase navigation, topic search, reading controls, practice and sources support that route.
