These are **findings for the owner's review**, not approved corrections. The original Markdown is unchanged and nothing has been added to `content/corrections-*.json`.

**Verification method:** MX2 was previously compiled with Kotlin 2.4.21. FL4 was independently compiled with Kotlin 2.4.21 and coroutines 1.11.0 during the follow-up review. ## Follow-up review disposition

The original chapters remain byte-identical. Each confirmed original finding is flagged before every affected H2 section listed above; notes identify the passage without stating the replacement. The MX2 compiler-rejection sentence from the active animation is held in `pending-android-phase4-teaching.blend` until approval. The StateFlow checkpoint now promises an atomic update without caller-managed locking rather than lock-free internals, matching [StateFlow's implementation notes](https://kotlinlang.org/api/kotlinx.coroutines/kotlinx-coroutines-core/kotlinx.coroutines.flow/-state-flow/#implementation-notes).

The foreground-service sequence now states its Android 14+/target-34+ location scenario and target-31+ background-start boundary. Its expedited-work checkpoint describes a scheduling request with quota/load and out-of-quota policy, not immediate execution. The Looper explanation distinguishes one scheduling API from all possible crossings and distinguishes Main from Main.immediate. The WhileSubscribed animation describes avoiding an upstream restart during a short subscriber gap without claiming Room cannot independently re-query. The job-tree animation states that cancellation is cooperative. The plain suspendCoroutine checkpoint distinguishes a cancelled Job from completion of a non-cancellable suspension and from API-specific listener cleanup.

The Doze illustration explicitly describes non-exempt app behaviour and non-exhaustive special cases; it no longer claims all unfinished work or every app must wait. Exact device schedules, manufacturer behaviour and real-device FCM delivery were not measured. The original's specific rate/quota statements should always be interpreted against the linked version-specific power-management documentation, not as an independent timing guarantee.
