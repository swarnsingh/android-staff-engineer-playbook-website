# Leadership reader rollout

## Scope and source

The learning path and all 5 chapters match the merged playbook byte for byte at [source PR #81](https://github.com/swarnsingh/android-staff-engineer-playbook/pull/81), commit `45201470a80d644a12eac0d9ff787e34f09062a2`. The website records the hashes of all 6 source files and their provenance in `source-snapshot.json`. The existing reader preserves all 40 original Q&A pairs.

Follow the original guided order through incident response, design review, influence, mentorship and escalation. The first phase starts with the on-call chapter and the second with influence across teams. The phase cards open their first lessons. The chapter order, examples and source links are preserved.

## Teaching layer

The openings name a concrete operational or cross-team decision. Each note helps the reader apply the source chapter to a specific outcome, such as assigning incident verification, recording an RFC decision, creating a chance to practise or routing an escalation to the person who can decide.

The incident figure follows detection through mitigation, verification, postmortem and follow-up. Its time and severity choices are marked as example team policy. The influence figure shows the sample reporting relationships. Its caption explains how to use the chart to locate the people who own decisions across teams. Each figure uses the shared design system and preserves the source diagram in a disclosure.
