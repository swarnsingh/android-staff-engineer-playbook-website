# Career

## Purpose

The Senior → Staff → Principal progression as a real, navigable path. What changes between levels, how to know when you've actually reached the next one, and how to position the work that gets you there.

## Why It Matters

Most engineers don't get to Staff by being better at coding. They get there by changing what work they choose, what they optimize for, and what they make visible.
